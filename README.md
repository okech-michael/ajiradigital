 Ajira Digital Club – Kisii University

Ajira Digital Club Kisii University is a student-led initiative aligned with the **Ajira Digital Program by the Government of Kenya.  
Our mission is to equip university students with **digital skills**, **online work experience, and career mentorship — preparing them for success in the global digital economy.

 Overview

The Ajira Digital Club Website serves as the official online platform for the club.  
It showcases our leadership team, member success stories, upcoming events, and collaboration opportunities with clients or organizations seeking skilled digital workers.

The platform aims to bridge the gap between **training and earning**, helping students transition from learning digital skills to securing real online gigs.

✨ Key Features

- Modern Landing Page: A clean, responsive design highlighting the club’s mission and vision.  
- Leaders Page: Profiles of the club’s executive members, including photos and bios.  
- Success Stories Page: Real stories of members who have earned or built careers online.  
- Client Work Portal: A space for clients to post jobs and connect directly with trained members.  
- Registration Page: Allows new members to sign up and join the club.  
- Admin Dashboard (optional): For managing posts, members, and job submissions.
